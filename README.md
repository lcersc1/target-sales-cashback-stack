## The Rules That Decide Whether Your Cashback Actually Pays

Cashback portals are simple to use and easy to fumble. Three mechanics decide whether the 2% shows up in your account:

1. **It's calculated on the discounted, pre-tax amount.** Cashback applies after sale prices and coupons are deducted, before sales tax. A $100 jacket marked down to $60 earns 2% of $60, not $100.
2. **The tracking session is fragile.** Start with a fresh browser tab, click through from TopCashback, and complete the purchase in that same session. Adding items to a cart you built earlier, or wandering off to another site mid-checkout, can break the tracking.
3. **Payment takes patience.** Cashback typically shows as "pending" within days of the order, gets confirmed after the retailer's return window closes, and becomes payable up to around 90 days later. Third-party guides consistently describe this timeline; it's the same at every portal because the retailer holds the commission until returns clear.

None of that requires effort once the click-through is done. It just means you shouldn't plan to spend the 2% the same afternoon.
